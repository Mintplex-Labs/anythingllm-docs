# Appearance Customization

<Callout type="info" emoji="️💡">
  **DOCKER VERSION ONLY!**

    These settings are only available in the Docker version of AnythingLLM

</Callout>

AnythingLLM allows you to customize the look and feel of your instance to match your brand and identity.

<Image
  src="/images/features/customization/appearance-settings-page.png"
  height={1080}
  width={1920}
  quality={100}
  alt="Appearance Settings Page"
/>

These settings live on the **Branding & Whitelabeling** page under **Settings → Customization**, grouped into tabs.

## Custom Logo

<Image
  src="/images/features/customization/custom-logo.png"
  height={1080}
  width={1920}
  quality={100}
  alt="Custom Logo"
/>

On the **Identity** tab, you can replace the AnythingLLM branded logo that appears on the login page and throughout the app with your own brand's logo. In this example, we have used a green square image for demonstration purposes.

## Custom Footer Links and Icons

<Image
  src="/images/features/customization/custom-footer-links-and-icons.png"
  height={1080}
  width={1920}
  quality={100}
  alt="Custom Footer Links and Icons"
/>

On the **Sidebar** tab, the footer icons can be replaced with custom links and icons to provide quick access to relevant resources or web pages.
