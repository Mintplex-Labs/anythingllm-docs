# Customization & Branding

AnythingLLM lets you change how the app looks, from the theme and colors you work in to, on Docker, the name, logo and links your whole team sees.

## UI Preferences

Open **Settings → Customization → UI Preferences**. These settings are personal: they change the app for you, not for anyone else.

### Theme

Choose **Light**, **Dark**, or **System** to follow your operating system's setting.

### Color palettes

A color palette gives the whole app your colors: the background, sidebar, chat and settings, in both light and dark mode. Pick one of the built-in palettes, including **True Dark** with pure black backgrounds, or click **New palette** to create your own from two colors:

- a **main color**, which tints the background, sidebar and content
- an **accent**, for buttons and links

The whole app previews a palette while you pick its colors. Edit or delete your palettes at any time.

- **Desktop:** the palette you click is the one the app uses. The assistant and Magic Beacon windows and the boot screen use it too. Available since [v1.19.0](/changelog/v1.19.0#color-palettes).
- **Docker:** you pick from the built-in palettes and the ones your admin has created. Admins create palettes, and can choose one for everyone, on the [Colors](#colors-docker-only) tab of Branding & Whitelabeling.

### Display language

Choose the language AnythingLLM's interface is shown in, where translations are available.

### Your name (Desktop only)

The name AnythingLLM greets you with on the home screen. Leave it blank to turn off personalized greetings.

### Default window (Desktop only)

Choose what opens when AnythingLLM starts: the main window, the assistant, or nothing, so it starts silently in the background.

## Branding & Whitelabeling (Docker only)

<Image
  src="/images/features/customization/appearance-settings-page.png"
  height={1080}
  width={1920}
  quality={100}
  alt="Branding & Whitelabeling settings"
/>

Admins and managers can brand an AnythingLLM instance for everyone who uses it. Open **Settings → Customization → Branding & Whitelabeling**. Settings are grouped into tabs:

| Tab                                 | What you can change                                 |
| ----------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- |
| [**Identity**](#identity-docker-only) | The app name shown on the login page, and your logo |
| [**Colors**](#colors-docker-only)     | The color palettes for the instance                 |
| [**Sidebar**](#sidebar-docker-only)   | The footer links and icons, and the support email   |
| [**Browser**](#browser-docker-only)   | The browser tab title and favicon                   |

### Identity (Docker only)

<Image
  src="/images/features/customization/custom-logo.png"
  height={1080}
  width={1920}
  quality={100}
  alt="Custom logo on the Identity tab"
/>

Set the app name shown on the login page, and replace the AnythingLLM logo on the login page and throughout the app with your own. In this example, a green square image is used for demonstration purposes.

### Colors (Docker only)

<Image
  src="/images/features/customization/colors-tab.png"
  height={1080}
  width={1920}
  quality={100}
  alt="Colors tab"
/>

Create the [color palettes](#color-palettes) everyone on the instance can choose from, and click a palette to make it the default for everyone. Changes save as soon as you make them.

**Enforce the default palette** makes everyone use the default palette. When it's off, users can pick their own palette under **UI Preferences** or in their account settings.

### Sidebar (Docker only)

<Image
  src="/images/features/customization/custom-footer-links-and-icons.png"
  height={1080}
  width={1920}
  quality={100}
  alt="Custom footer links and icons"
/>

Replace the footer icons with your own links and icons, to give quick access to relevant resources or web pages. You can also set the support email users see.

### Browser (Docker only)

Set the title and favicon shown on the browser tab.
